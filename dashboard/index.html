Max pain by expiration

The price where option holders as a group would lose the most at each expiration. Some traders watch for drift toward it into expiry; it is not a price target.

Gamma by expiration

Which expirations carry the most gamma. A large near-term share means today's and this week's options dominate the levels, and they can shift quickly.
