Install or update (about 1 minute)
  1. Click Copy code.
  2. In TradingView open the Pine Editor (bottom panel). First time: Open > New indicator. Updating: open GEX PRO.
  3. Select everything (Ctrl+A), paste (Ctrl+V), then Save.
  4. First time only: Add to chart on your NQ and ES charts.

A new paste every hour on the hour (ET). Click below, then in TradingView: indicator settings > Levels payload > Ctrl+A, Ctrl+V > OK.
