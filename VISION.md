# GEX PRO vision

Written 2026-11-19. Change only with a dated reason at the bottom.

## Who it is for
An NQ / ES futures trader who opens the site before and during the cash session, often on a phone, and draws the
levels on a TradingView chart. They know what a call wall is; they don't want to read a paper to use the page.

## The three questions they bring every morning
1. **Where will price react?** C1 / P1 / Ab1, the flip, the walls, in futures prices, with how far each is.
2. **Which side has the edge?** The gamma regime and what it implies (fade vs follow), and only claims of edge that a
   pre-registered study backs, with n. "No proven edge yet" is a valid answer and is said plainly.
3. **What changed?** Since yesterday's close and since the open: which levels moved, when, by how much.

## What the site must answer in 5 seconds
The Levels page, top to bottom: the price and its time, the regime, the key levels with distances, today's plan
(which zone price is in), and the paste for TradingView. Everything else is one tap away, not in the way.

## What every number carries
Its "as of" time, its source, and for a statistic its sample size. Missing data says "unavailable" and why. Old data
never looks current. Estimates are labelled as estimates.

## What it refuses to be
- Cluttered: a card nobody would miss is removed, not restyled.
- Hype: no "signals", no win-rate marketing, no colour used for excitement.
- Advice: no orders, no recommendations; simulated trades are labelled hypothetical.
- Paid: free data only, and it must work from GitHub's servers.
- Fragile: the paste format and the installed indicator keep working across updates.

## How it should feel
Calm and exact. One colour per meaning (calls, puts, the flip, warnings), numbers aligned, explanations behind "?".
The same on a 375 px phone in light mode as on a 1366 px desktop in dark mode.
